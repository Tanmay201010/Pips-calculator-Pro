from the saved stories

Keeping the knots happy.

Macramé is forgiving. Four small habits keep it looking new for years, and years is the plan.

care notes no. 1 to 4

Dust, softly.

Once a month, a soft brush or a cool hairdryer on low. Never a damp cloth, and never a scrubbing hand.

Sunlight, in moderation.

Cotton enjoys the light, but keep strongly dyed cords out of harsh afternoon sun, or the colour will slowly walk away.

Comb the fringe.

Small pulls happen. Smooth them back with a wide-tooth comb, one section at a time, from the bottom down.

No machines, ever.

Spot clean only. If something spills, blot it, then let it air dry away from heat. Knots deserve better than a spin cycle.

The questions I get most.

Anything else, ask me straight in the DMs. I answer every message myself, Monday to Saturday.

Ask me anything
Can I ask for a custom size or colour?

Almost always yes. Most wall hangings can be made to any width, and cords come in natural, sand, rose, terracotta and near-black. Custom shades need about a week’s notice.

How long does a piece take?

Charms and keyrings take two to three days. Wall hangings take five to ten, including drying and photographs. I always confirm the date before you pay.

Do you ship outside India?

Yes, tracked. Most international parcels arrive in seven to fourteen days, and any customs duties are paid by the receiver. I pack everything to survive the journey.

Do you take bulk or corporate orders?

Yes, from ten pieces up. I do branded tags, matched dye lots and can hit a fixed event date if you message at least three weeks ahead.

What if it arrives damaged?

Send me a photo within 48 hours and I’ll repair or remake it. In four years of shipping, I’ve only had to do it twice.

Is it safe around pets and children?

Cotton cord and oak dowel, so no sharp edges anywhere. Keep beaded charms and dreamcatchers with feathers out of small hands and curious mouths.

Tell me its size, its light and the colours you keep coming back to. We’ll take it from there, one knot at a time.
