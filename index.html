Your Questions Answered

How does QUANTUM calculate MT5 Lot Sizes? +
QUANTUM calculates position sizing based on your Account Balance, Risk Percentage, and Stop Loss Price Delta. It automatically multiplies by contract sizing rules (e.g. 100,000 for standard Forex, 1,000 for JPY, 100 for Gold, 1 for Crypto).
Can I copy the output directly into MetaTrader 5? +
Yes! Clicking "Copy Lots" exports the calculated lot size straight to your clipboard so you can paste it into MT5 order dialogue boxes instantly.
Does it support Gold (XAUUSD) and JPY pairs? +
Yes, selecting Gold automatically switches to 100 oz contract sizing, and JPY pairs apply normalized 1,000-unit scaling.
